# Functionality  

As oldheads mightve inferred from the (very original) name, this is a design inspired by the [di:stage](https://myfigurecollection.net/item/53036), which Max Factory made from 2009 to 2014.  

To get them 12 years later, you're left to the pre-owned market, where the prices go from [reasonable](https://www.amiami.com/eng/detail/?gcode=FIG-IPN-0552-R) to [scalper bullshit](https://www.ebay.com/itm/265581986604)... And now that 3D printing is mainstream-ish there's really no reason for people to not just _print their own figure stands_.  

This [blogpost from the era](https://arcticukitsu.com/Blog/?p=262) explains how it works pretty well, and a lot of it also applies to the difStage.  

![A difStage set, in marble PLA](/images/difstage/parts.jpg)  
A set comes with:  

- one base stand   
- a connector part to place another stand next to one of the four sides  
- another connector part to place a separate stand, except vertically  
- four "skirt" parts to hide the sides you're not connecting anything to  

If you do happen to own an old di:stage, it is compatible with the difStage as long as you still use the OG connector parts with it.  
![A combination of two difStages and one di:stage, forming a 3D diorama scene with two walls](/images/difstage/single_fig.jpg)  

# Development   

I was a bit surprised that nobody did this yet<sup id="ref-2">[**](#note-2)</sup> at first, but after getting into it I realized that:  
A) damn CAD is hard<sup id="ref-3">[***](#note-3)</sup>  
B) while this is inspired by the di:stage, you can't just 1:1 redo it as the design doesn't really lend itself well to 3D printing.  

Getting this in a state where it's usable and doesn't break certainly took a few attempts.. I'm not entirely satisfied by the connector pegs which are still a bit brittle when unplugging parts, but that can be alleviated by tweaking the print settings.  

It's still a thing I'd like to improve in an eventual V2; Although the current system has the advantage of being backwards compatible with the di:stage somewhat.    
![difStage skirt parts on a di:stage because why not](/images/difstage/interop.jpg)  
In the future I'd also like to keep experimenting with the system and make a few more connectors/stand variants:  
Ideas include larger stands, a connector to attach a stage to the [Ikea pegboard](https://www.ikea.com/be/en/cat/skadis-series-37813/), a riser/stacking system similar to what the OG [di:stage Expansion Set](https://myfigurecollection.net/item/53038) did, and support for different peg hole diameters.  

I thought about selling the 3D models after seeing the [Camera+](https://ko-fi.com/s/9457d1cc6e) Gameboy camera shell do the same thing.  
Similar to that, if I make any addons in the future I'll add them to the 3D model download for free!
