Introducing the difStage: A modular 3D-printable figure stand

Thu 01 October 2026
By Difegue
Category: 3D Printing
Tags: figma revoltech 3d print di:stage action figure stand

Print 10 of them, chain them up, make the epic battle fanfic of your dreams! Print another 10 and make a school AU afterwards!

I own a bunch of anime action figures. I really got into Max Factory's figmas back in 2012 or so, alongside the other 1/12 figures like S.H.Figuarts or Revoltechs*.

The entire point of having your blorbos in 1/12 scale is to make up silly little scenes with them, which is always a bit annoying if you have to use the provided individual stands for each one of them. Here's a solution you can make at home!

I apologize for my posing being dogshit. it will happen again

The difStage is a 3D-printable stand you can use to pose multiple 1/12 figures at once and most importantly, combine with others to make even larger stands. Scroll for more info, or just click the links below:

Functionality

As oldheads mightve inferred from the (very original) name, this is a design inspired by the di:stage, which Max Factory made from 2009 to 2014.

To get them 12 years later, you're left to the pre-owned market, where the prices go from reasonable to scalper bullshit... And now that 3D printing is mainstream-ish there's really no reason for people to not just print their own figure stands.

This blogpost from the era explains how it works pretty well, and a lot of it also applies to the difStage.


A set comes with:

  • one base stand (10x10cm)
  • a connector part to place another stand next to one of the four sides
  • another connector part to place a separate stand, except vertically
  • four "skirt" parts to hide the sides you're not connecting anything to

If you do happen to own an old di:stage, it is compatible with the difStage as long as you still use the OG connector parts with it.

Development

I was a bit surprised that nobody did this yet** at first, but after getting into it I realized that:
A) damn CAD is hard***
B) while this is inspired by the di:stage, you can't just 1:1 redo it as the design doesn't really lend itself well to 3D printing.

Getting this in a state where it's usable and doesn't break certainly took a few attempts.. I'm not entirely satisfied by the connector pegs which are still a bit brittle when unplugging parts, but that can be alleviated by tweaking the print settings.

It's still a thing I'd like to improve in an eventual V2; Although the current system has the advantage of being backwards compatible with the di:stage somewhat.

In the future I'd also like to keep experimenting with the system and make a few more connectors/stand variants:
Ideas include larger stands, a connector to attach a stage to the Ikea pegboard, a riser/stacking system similar to what the OG di:stage Expansion Set did, and support for different peg hole diameters.

I thought about selling the 3D models after seeing the Camera+ Gameboy camera shell do the same thing.
Similar to that, if I make any addons in the future I'll add them to the 3D model download for free!

* Revos are the most fun to pose action figures you can get despite the sculpts being a bit awkward-looking (and even then, from 2020 onwards their quality is about the same as figma imo) and I'll always vouch for them - The Revoltech Evangelion units are probably among my favorite action figures.
** Unless someone did but I didn't find it, which would be very baka of me.
